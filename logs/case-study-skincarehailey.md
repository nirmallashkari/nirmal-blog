# Case Study — skincarehailey.com (Website + Pinterest + Instagram in 72 Hours)

**Live Site:** https://skincarehailey.com  
**Scope:** Full-Stack Web Build, Technical SEO, Affiliate Integration & Social Traffic Pipeline  
**Timeline:** 3 Days (72 Hours)

---

## What Was Built & Shipped

### 1. Editorial Next.js Website (`skincarehailey.com`)
- Redesigned the homepage hero, navigation bar, editorial post cards, and footer for fast mobile-first reading.
- Structured 43 long-form skincare, haircare, style, and lifestyle comparison guides (`/guides` and `/blog/[slug]`).

### 2. Affiliate & Link Integrity
- Integrated verified Amazon Associate product cards and live links across guides, eliminating broken 404 links across mixed posts.
- Added `/links` hub and full affiliate compliance disclosures.

### 3. Technical SEO & AI Discovery
- Configured dynamic `sitemap.ts`, `robots.ts`, and `llms.txt` for search engines and AI crawlers.
- Built `/about`, `/contact`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, and `/disclaimer` pages.

### 4. Multi-Channel Growth Pipeline
- Connected **Pinterest** (`pinterest.com/haileymorgan_28`) pin creation and **Instagram** (`instagram.com/haileymorgan_28`) content flows directly into high-intent site guides.
